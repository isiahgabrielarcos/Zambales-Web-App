🌊 Anawangin Cove Camping Guidelines Updated for 2026

The Zambales Tourism Office has released updated camping guidelines for Anawangin, Nagsasa, and Silanguin Coves effective July 1, 2026. Key changes include a new cap of 150 campers per cove per night to reduce environmental pressure, mandatory leave-no-trace compliance, and a new digital registration system at the Pundaquit jump-off point.

All visitors must register and pay the ₱50 environmental fee before boarding pumpboats. Advance online registration is now available via the San Antonio Municipal Tourism office portal.

🥭 Singkaban Festival 2026 — Dates Announced!

The Singkaban Festival, the premier annual celebration of Iba and the province of Zambales, is set for August 14–22, 2026. This year's theme: "Zambales: Tahanan ng Pagmamahal at Pag-asa" (Zambales: Home of Love and Hope).

Highlights include:
• Opening salvo and float parade on August 14
• Street dancing competition on August 19
• Agri-trade fair and cultural exhibits throughout the week
• Grand coronation night on August 22

Full schedule to be posted on the official Zambales Provincial Government page.

🤿 New Dive Trail Launched in Subic Bay

Subic Bay Aquamarine Foundation (SBAF) in partnership with the Subic Bay Metropolitan Authority (SBMA) has launched a new Heritage Dive Trail connecting 5 of the bay's most historically significant WWII wreck sites. The trail includes underwater interpretive markers and a companion mobile guide accessible via QR codes placed at each dive site buoy.

The wrecks included: USS New York · El Capitan · LST-488 · YO-160 · Landing Craft. All at depths between 8 and 27 meters — accessible to both beginner and advanced divers. Contact SBMA Dive Operations for permits and boat hire.
