Mountains, Valleys & Plains

Landscapes of Zambales

The Terrain

Dramatic. Rugged. Alive.

The province of Zambales is defined by a dramatic geography — the Zambales mountain range to the east and the South China Sea to the west, with the lahar-sculpted plains of Mt. Pinatubo in between. Whether you're chasing peaks or rolling through alien lahar landscapes in a 4×4, the land itself is the adventure.

Must-Visit

Top 5 Land Destinations

1
Volcano · Tarlac/Zambales/Pampanga

Mt. Pinatubo & Crater Lake

One of the most remarkable hikes in Southeast Asia. A 4×4 ride across the otherworldly lahar plains is followed by a trek through river gorges to the stunning jade-green crater lake left by the 1991 eruption. Permits are required; tours depart from Capas, Tarlac, with many starting in Subic/Olongapo.

2
Mountain Range · Multiple Municipalities

Zambales Mountain Range

Stretching the length of the province, the Zambales Range features multiple peaks suitable for day hikes and multi-day mountaineering expeditions. Mt. Tapulao (High Peak), the highest mountain in the range at 2,037 m, is a favourite of serious trekkers for its mossy forests and sweeping views.

View on Google Maps
3
Geological Wonder · San Marcelino / San Antonio

Lahar Canyons of Pinatubo

The massive lahar flows from Mt. Pinatubo carved dramatic canyons through the foothills over the years. The 4×4 safari through these barren, Mars-like landscapes — cutting through canyon walls of compacted volcanic ash — is a must-do adventure unlike anything else in the country.

View on Google Maps
4
Hiking · Palauig

Mt. Tapulao (High Peak)

At 2,037 meters, Mt. Tapulao is the highest peak in Zambales and stands as a serious hiking challenge for outdoor enthusiasts. The summit trail passes through lush pine forests and elfin moss ecosystems rarely seen at lower elevations. Camping at the summit reveals sweeping views of the Lingayen Gulf and South China Sea on clear days.

View on Google Maps
5
Valley & Camping · San Antonio

Anawangin Cove & Valley

Though primarily known for its beach, Anawangin's inland valley — a surreal grove of Agoho pines growing on volcanic ash deposited by Pinatubo — is a landscape entirely unique to this corner of the world. Camping overnight under the pines and waking to mist rolling off the hills is an unforgettable experience.

Get Active

Land Activities

Camping

Camp under Agoho pines at Anawangin, on the Pinatubo lahar plains, or in the mountain highlands. Bring your own gear or rent locally.

Hiking & Trekking

From the easy ridgeline walks of Iba Eco Park to the serious multi-day summit of Mt. Tapulao, there's a trail for every fitness level.

4×4 Lahar Safari

The most iconic Pinatubo experience — open-air 4×4 trucks blasting through towering lahar canyons on the way to the crater lake trailhead.

Mountaineering

Mt. Tapulao and other peaks in the Zambales Range attract serious mountaineers. Register with the local MENRO or tourism office before ascending.
